# Installing Food Tracker

Android only.

1. Get the file `FoodTracker.apk` onto your phone (Quick Share, Google Drive, email, whatever).
2. Tap the file.
3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect warns you, tap **Install anyway**.
5. Open **Food Tracker**.

## Using it

- **First launch**: type your name and tap **Continue**.
- **Adding food**: tap the green **Add to …** button at the bottom of Today (it picks the meal by time of day; tap another meal at the top to change it), or **+** on a meal. Then type in **Search foods**. Common foods like "banana" or "eggs" show up right away: tap one, pick how much, and check the numbers before adding. Restaurant meals show up too: try "five guys cheeseburger", "jersey mikes turkey" or "triple dipper", or type a chain's name to see its menu. For packaged food, tap **Search online** (needs internet), or tap the barcode icon in the search box to scan. After adding something you stay on that screen, so you can add the rest of the meal; tap ← when you're done.
- **Today**: the rings at the top show how each goal is going. Tap any food you logged to change how much you had, move it to another meal, or remove it. Use the arrows at the top to look at other days. A ring turns orange when you hit that goal.
- **Same as yesterday?** If a meal is empty and you ate something for it yesterday, tap **Repeat yesterday** to log the same foods.
- **Notes**: at the bottom of Today there's a box for a short note about the day. There's no save button: it saves as you type, and a small **Saved** appears once it has. Tap **Done** (✓) on the keyboard when you're finished. Each day has its own note.
- **Daily limits** (optional): in **Goals**, switch on **Sodium** or **Added sugars** to see how close you are to the daily limit on Today. The bar turns red if you go over. Common foods fill these in for you; for anything else, copy them from the label (sodium in mg, and the "Includes … Added Sugars" line), or leave them blank if you don't know.
- **History**: switch between **Week** and **Month**. Each day's ring fills as you hit goals and turns orange when you hit them all; days you didn't log stay blank. Below that, a chart per goal shows each day against the goal line, how many days you hit it, your daily average, and your streak. Tap a day to see it.
- **Backup**: in **Goals**, tap the red **Save a backup** button now and then and save the file to Google Drive (or anywhere you like). On a new phone, or if the app ever gets uninstalled, tap **Restore from a backup** on the welcome screen (or **Restore** in Goals) and pick that file to get everything back.
- **My foods**: foods you've saved. Tap one to edit it. To add it again, tap **+** on a meal and pick it from the list.
- **Goals**: change your name, and set your sex, age and weight for your goals. You can also hide calories here, and switch off any goal you don't care about (say, fruit). A switched-off goal disappears from Today but is still counted, so switching it back on brings it all back.

Your data stays on your phone only.

## Updates

When I send you a new `FoodTracker.apk`, just install it the same way. Your data stays. Don't uninstall first: that deletes everything on the phone, unless you saved a backup (Goals → **Save a backup**) to restore from.

To see which version you have: phone **Settings → Apps → Food Tracker**.

**Updating to 0.1.13:** **Search foods** now has Jersey Mike's. Try "jersey mikes turkey", or type "jersey mikes" to see them all. Only the Regular size is listed, on white bread; cold subs are "Mike's Way" (with onions, lettuce, tomato, oil and vinegar). If you get a Mini, change **Servings** to about 0.6; for a Giant, about 1.8.

**Updating to 0.1.12:** a cleaner app icon. Same leaf-in-a-ring idea, but better balanced, so it no longer looks off-center. Some phones only show the new icon after a restart.

**Updating to 0.1.11:** behind-the-scenes tidying only; nothing looks different.

**Updating to 0.1.10:** backups! In **Goals**, tap the red **Save a backup** button and save the file to Google Drive (or anywhere). If you ever get a new phone or the app gets uninstalled, tap **Restore from a backup** when the app opens and pick that file, and everything comes back. Worth doing every week or two; the app shows when you last did it.

**Updating to 0.1.9:** quicker logging. Tap the green **Add to …** button at the bottom of Today; it picks the meal by the time of day, and you can switch meals at the top. After adding a food you stay on the search screen, so you can add the rest of the meal in a row. The rings are smaller, so all your meals fit on the first screen. Tap a food you logged to move it to another meal. The barcode scanner is now the barcode icon in the search box.

**Updating to 0.1.8:** **Search foods** now finds restaurant meals from Applebee's, Chick-fil-A, Chili's, Culver's, Five Guys, IHOP, Olive Garden, Taco Bell and Texas Roadhouse. Try "five guys cheeseburger". For a Chili's Triple Dipper, add each of your three picks. Restaurants don't say how much fruit or veg is in a meal, so add those yourself if your order had some.

**Updating to 0.1.7:** notes now say **Saved** when they're saved, and the keyboard's ✓ button closes the keyboard when you're done typing a note.

**Updating to 0.1.6:** optional daily limits for **sodium** and **added sugars**. Switch them on in the Goals tab to see a bar on Today that turns red if you go over. Foods you logged before won't have these numbers, so Today will say so. Also, the keyboard no longer hides the **Add** button when you type in a food.

**Updating to 0.1.5:** new **History** tab. See each day of the week or month as a ring, charts of each goal over time, and your streaks. Also, if the app was left open overnight, it now switches to the new day by itself.

**Updating to 0.1.4:** when adding food, type in **Search foods** to find common foods like banana or eggs, with the fruit and veg servings already filled in. Packaged foods can be found with **Search online**. In the Goals tab you can now switch off goals you don't want to see.

**Updating to 0.1.3:** at the bottom of the Goals tab, tap any source to open the official page each goal comes from.

**Updating to 0.1.2:** new icon and colors, **Repeat yesterday**, and rings that light up when you hit a goal. Coming from 1.0, the app also asks for your name once when you first open it. Your food log is still there either way. (Yes, 0.1.x is a lower number than 1.0 — I renumbered it, but it's newer.)
